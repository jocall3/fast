# Chapter 1: The Unseen Current

In a world measured by the relentless tick of clocks, where moments are counted and destinies charted by the passage of time, there whispers a force. It is older than the mountains, swifter than light, and more elusive than a dream forgotten upon waking.

Some call it 'pace,' others 'velocity,' or merely 'speed.' But those who have truly encountered its essence, who have felt its pull and witnessed its breathtaking dance across the fabric of existence, know a different name. They speak of 'fast.'

It is not merely a measure of how quickly one point gives way to another. No. 'Fast' is the current beneath the surface, the unseen hand that turns the world, the silent roar that shapes all things. It is the architect of fleeting beauty and sudden ruin, the whisper of opportunity and the gasp of loss.

It touches everything, yet remains untouchable. It is in the blinding flash of a falling star, the silent rush of a thought, the sudden decay of empires, and the miraculous bloom of life in a barren land. It grants power beyond comprehension, but demands a price only the most daring are willing to pay.

'Fast' does not merely move; it transforms. It does not merely arrive; it manifests. It is the sudden clarity in chaos, the swift answer to an unasked question, the precipice between what was and what will never be again.

Prepare yourself, for you stand at the threshold of understanding. What is 'fast'? It is the beginning, the end, and the journey that devours them both. It is the mystery we are about to unravel. And once seen, it cannot be unseen.
