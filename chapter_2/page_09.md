The pervasive influence of 'Fast' extends beyond the observable universe, reaching into the very core of our internal experience. It is within the intricate tapestry of subjective time, rather than the relentless tick of a cosmic clock, that 'Fast' reveals one of its most profound manipulations. Here, it does not merely accelerate or decelerate events, but reshapes the very duration of existence as we perceive it, twisting moments into eternities and eternities into mere blips.

When the current of joy surges through us, 'Fast' acts as a potent amplifier, compressing the most exquisite moments into dazzling, ephemeral flashes. A lifetime of happiness can feel like a single, radiant breath, swiftly drawn and exhaled. In these instances, 'Fast' doesn't steal time, but enriches its density, making each millisecond vibrant with an intensity that defies linear measurement. It is the brilliant, fleeting arc of a meteor across the night sky – spectacular, unforgettable, yet gone before its full trajectory can be traced, leaving behind only the ghost of its passage and an ache for its return.

Conversely, when the soul is gripped by the exquisite agony of anticipation, 'Fast' shifts its tempo to an agonizing crawl. The hands of the internal clock seem to seize, each second expanding into a vast, unyielding epoch. A single night can stretch into an eternity, heavy with unspoken possibilities or dread. Here, 'Fast' is not a sprinter but a sculptor of delay, meticulously drawing out each moment, making us dwell in the precipice of what is to come. It transforms waiting into a profound landscape, where every unfulfilled desire and every imagined outcome casts a long, drawn-out shadow, teaching us the profound weight of time's unyielding stretch.

Thus, 'Fast' orchestrates the very rhythm of our consciousness, proving itself not just a force of external change but a master conductor of our internal temporal landscape. It can compress joy into an explosive brevity or expand expectation into an infinite horizon, demonstrating its intimate dominion over the most personal and elusive of all dimensions: the time we feel, the time we live.
